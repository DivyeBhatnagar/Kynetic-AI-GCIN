## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, editorial, and restrained aesthetic tailored for high-scale enterprise compute orchestration and GPU optimization. Moving decisively away from ubiquitous cyber-neon palettes, synthetic violet glows, and high-saturation tech gradients, the visual language draws inspiration from industrial design monographs, brutalist Swiss typography, and high-end physical hardware consoles.

The personality balances high-stakes engineering rigor with the quiet confidence of an established institutional platform. The emotional response is one of absolute control, low thermal stress, and technical precision. The visual movement fuses **Editorial Minimalism** with **Structured Monastic Utilitarianism**: warm paper-like ivory substrates, sharp hairline borders, disciplined neutral typography, and deliberate deep olive and muted lime accents that communicate system health without visual noise.

## Colors

The color system operates on an architectural, physical-paper discipline. Surfaces avoid synthetic harsh whites or pitch-black contrast in favor of warm, tactile tonal layers.

- **Primary Canvas (`canvas-base`, #F8F7F3)**: A warm natural ivory tone providing a calm, non-glare foundation across large-format displays.
- **Secondary Alternating Canvas (`canvas-alternate`, #F0EFE9)**: Applied to compute telemetry panels, dense data tables, and alternating page sections to segment modules without heavy line dividers.
- **Surface Elevated (`surface-elevated`, #FFFFFF)**: Pure white is reserved solely for raised inspection cards, interactive flyouts, and active input containers.
- **Ink Palette (`#191919`, `#4E4E4A`, `#686861`)**: Near-black charcoal typography ensures high legibility while avoiding optical vibration against warm surfaces. Body copy uses `#4E4E4A` for effortless reading of dense operational documentation.
- **Hardware Accents (`#59664D`, `#84A96C`, `#65A30D`)**: Deep olive conveys structural permanence and engineering maturity. Muted lime and status greens are restricted exclusively to operational telemetry: cluster health status indicators, node activity dots, and run state confirmations.
- **Borders & Rules (`#DEDDD5`, `#E8E7E0`)**: Precise hairline dividers create structural grid boundaries reminiscent of technical blueprints.

## Typography

The typographic system pairs the razor-sharp, geometric geometry of **Geist** for display headlines, metrics, and technical labeling with the balanced utility of **Inter** for running copy and high-density metadata.

- **Display Headlines**: Geist rendered in weights 400 and 500 with tight line-heights (1.05 to 1.1x) and aggressive negative tracking (-0.02em to -0.035em). Headlines feel carved and architectural rather than decorative.
- **Body Text**: Inter provides fatigue-free reading for architectural cluster configuration, GPU metrics, and workload execution logs with natural spacing and 1.5x leading.
- **Technical Labels & Monospace Telemetry**: Geist handles micro-labels and technical data tags using uppercase tracking (+0.04em to +0.06em) to deliver an instrument-panel feel.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an architectural 12-column modular grid built on a strict 4px base increment. 

- **Grid Architecture**: 12 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters on desktop screens (>1024px), transitioning to 8 columns on tablet viewports (640px–1023px) with 16px gutters, and 4 columns on mobile devices (<640px).
- **Rhythm & Cadence**: Major content bands are separated by disciplined `space-section` (96px / 6rem) intervals, reinforcing editorial breathing room. Compact operational dashboards use dense `space-xs` (8px) and `space-sm` (12px) clustering to keep high-frequency node states glanceable.
- **Section Enclosures**: Rather than full-bleed card carousels, layouts align strictly to max container boundaries of 1240px, anchored with subtle warm-grey vertical guide borders at viewport margins.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this design system is architectural and tactile, eschewing diffuse floating shadows and heavy blur layers. Hierarchy is established strictly through tonal substrate contrast and structural hairlines:

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - **Ground Floor (Base Canvas)**: `#F8F7F3` serves as the primary canvas plane.
  - **Recessed Inset (Alternate Canvas)**: `#F0EFE9` denotes nested monitoring clusters, terminal readouts, and tabular data wells.
  - **Raised Level (Elevated Card)**: `#FFFFFF` paired with a 1px border (`#DEDDD5`) distinguishes actionable cards, command modals, and context panels.
- **Shadowless Construction**: Ambient drop shadows are prohibited in resting states. Elevation is signaled purely by the 1px `#DEDDD5` hairline edge against the `#F8F7F3` or `#F0EFE9` background.
- **Hover & Interaction Lift**: When interactive cards receive cursor focus, they do not blur outward; they gain a crisp inner offset border (`#C5C4BA`) accompanied by a subtle, physically grounded tint shift (`#FFFFFF` to `#FCFAF6`).

## Shapes

The shape philosophy is understated, engineered, and disciplined. 

- **Base Radius (4px)**: Applied to input fields, contextual data tiles, telemetry tags, and structural sub-panels.
- **Card Radius (6px to 8px)**: Applied to operational cards, cluster topology containers, and modal frames to soften high-density displays without looking playful.
- **Pill Geometry (9999px)**: Reserved deliberately and exclusively for primary CTA buttons, compact status badges, and chip filters. This stark contrast between rectangular architectural frames and pill-shaped action triggers directs user focus intuitively to interaction points.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Pill-shaped (`roundedness: 3` / 9999px), background `#191919`, text `#F8F7F3` (`label-mono`, 13px, weight 500). Height 40px, horizontal padding 20px. Hover state shifts background to `#3E4736` (deep olive).
- **Secondary Outline**: Pill-shaped, transparent background, 1px solid `#DEDDD5`, text `#191919`. Hover state shifts border to `#191919` with subtle `#F0EFE9` background fill.
- **Compact Technical Action**: Corner radius 4px, height 32px, padding 8px 12px, border 1px solid `#DEDDD5`, background `#FFFFFF`. Monospace metric labels.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Node & Telemetry Status**: Pill-shaped, padding 4px 10px, background `#F0EFE9`, border 1px solid `#DEDDD5`. Incorporates a live 6px circular indicator dot: `#65A30D` (Active), `#84A96C` (Idle/Healthy), `#B45309` (Thermal Throttled), `#991B1B` (Failed).
- **Cluster Category Filter**: Height 28px, radius 4px, uppercase `label-tag`, subtle neutral text `#4E4E4A`. Selected state uses `#191919` text and `#DEDDD5` background.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Radius 4px, height 40px, padding 10px 14px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#DEDDD5`. Focus state converts border to 1px `#191919` with a subtle 2px `#E8E7E0` outer halo (no colorful glow).
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 16px square (checkbox, radius 3px) or 16px circle (radio). 1px border `#DEDDD5`. Checked state fills `#191919` with `#F8F7F3` glyph.

### Cards & Telemetry Containers
- **Compute Cluster Card**: Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px hairline border `#DEDDD5`, radius 8px, padding 24px. Header features node architecture title (`title-md`) alongside live status pill. Divided internally by 1px `#E8E7E0` horizontal lines.
- **Console / Telemetry Well**: Inset background `#F0EFE9`, radius 6px, border 1px solid `#E8E7E0`, padding 16px. Geist Monospace readouts with muted metadata headers (`#686861`).

### Lists & Data Rows
- **Resource Allocation Rows**: Border-bottom 1px solid `#E8E7E0`, padding 12px 0. Hover state subtly changes canvas to `#F4F3ED`. Left-aligned identifier, right-aligned allocation percentages and GPU load figures in tabular figures (`tnum`).
