## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, editorial, and restrained aesthetic engineered for high-performance compute infrastructure and technical platforms. Departing from dark sci-fi tropes, saturated synthetics, and heavy terminal glow, the visual language balances the tangible tactility of fine industrial print monographs with the clarity of precision aerospace instrumentation.

The personality balances computational rigor with quiet confidence. The emotional response evokes total command, crisp operational focus, and thermal calm. The visual style merges **Editorial Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Technical Precision**: warm off-white and ivory substrates, hairline structural framing, disciplined neutral typography, and luminous, refined light blue accents that evoke clear sky, cool airflow, and ambient optical depth without introducing neon distraction.

## Colors

The color system operates on physical ivory substrates enriched with refined sky and ice blue gradients that convey active cooling, computational flow, and visual breathability.

- **Primary Canvas (`canvas-base`, #F8F7F3)**: Warm, glare-free linen paper tone creating a tactile ground plane across high-density consoles.
- **Secondary Alternating Canvas (`canvas-alternate`, #F0EFE9)**: Applied to telemetry wells, alternating record rows, and nested architectural blocks.
- **Surface Elevated (`surface-elevated`, #FFFFFF)**: Pure white reserved strictly for actionable modal panels, elevated cards, and active input fields.
- **Atmospheric Blue Gradient Suite**: 
  - Subdued Sky Tint (`#E0F2FE`) and Soft Sky (`#BAE6FD`) provide ethereal background sweeps behind hero metrics and structural headers.
  - Ice Blue (`#93C5FD`, `#60A5FA`) delivers clear, readable status indicators, focus boundaries, and selected states.
  - Electric Sky Blue (`#3B82F6`) serves as the signature focal accent for primary interactive highlights, progress indicators, and active node status.
- **Ink Palette (`#191919`, `#4E4E4A`, `#686861`, `#9E9D95`)**: Dense charcoal values guarantee optical clarity against both warm ivory and atmospheric blue washes without stark vibration.
- **Hairlines & Boundaries (`#DEDDD5`, `#E8E7E0`, `#C5C4BA`)**: Blueprint-grade borders providing structural rhythm.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy leverages **Geist** for crisp structural headings and technical metrics, balanced with **Inter** for sustained legibility across dense documentation and telemetry tables.

- **Display & Headings**: Geist headlines feature compact line spacing and subtle negative kerning (-0.02em to -0.035em), creating an architectural silhouette.
- **Body Text**: Inter delivers comfortable optical spacing and rhythmic vertical line progression (1.5x to 1.55x line height) across cluster readouts and descriptive technical copy.
- **Labels & Telemetry**: Geist handles technical indicators, numerical cluster counters, and tags using wide uppercase tracking (+0.04em to +0.06em) and tabular numerals (`tnum`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a strict 12-column architectural grid grounded in 4px baseline multiples.

- **Grid Dynamics**: Desktop layouts (>1024px) utilize a 12-column configuration with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and max content enclosures of 1240px. Tablet screens (640px–1023px) adjust to 8 columns with 16px gutters, while mobile displays (<640px) condense to a 4-column system.
- **Spacing Cadence**: Micro-spacing (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) clusters tightly coupled telemetry readouts and badge groups. Macro-spacing (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) enforces generous structural breathing room between unrelated hardware modules and page headers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through structural surface layering, hairline delineation, and subtle atmospheric light blue gradients rather than diffuse heavy drop shadows.

- **Atmospheric Gradient Overlays**: Key hero headers, active metric spotlights, and selected panel headers introduce an ambient wash (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(224, 242, 254, 0.45) 0%, rgba(248, 247, 243, 0) 100%)`). This imparts a sense of diffuse skylight and thermal dissipation across the warm ivory canvas.
- **Tiers of Ground**:
  - **Base Ground (`#F8F7F3`)**: Neutral warm canvas.
  - **Recessed Wells (`#F0EFE9`)**: Inset containers, data logs, and nested cluster monitors.
  - **Elevated White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white cards anchored with a 1px `#DEDDD5` outline.
- **Focus & Selection Lift**: Elevated interactive surfaces gain a luminous ice blue edge highlight (`1px solid #93C5FD`) paired with an ultra-subtle, directional tint (`rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.04)`), replacing conventional dark elevation shadows with an optical emission effect.

## Shapes

The geometry reflects deliberate industrial restraint: compact rectangular forms with softened edges.

- **Structural Elements (4px to 6px)**: Form fields, nested data tiles, cards, and modal frames use disciplined, low-radius geometry to preserve clean horizontal and vertical grid alignment.
- **Interactive Triggers & Indicators (Pill / 9999px)**: Full pill geometry is reserved intentionally for actionable buttons, category filters, and live node status badges, providing unmistakable contrast against rectangular data matrices.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Full pill shape, solid `#191919` surface, text `#FFFFFF` using `label-mono` (13px, weight 500). Height 40px, horizontal padding 20px. Hover state applies an atmospheric gradient or shifts to an electric sky transition with `#1D4ED8` accent trim.
- **Secondary Action**: Pill shape, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#DEDDD5`, text `#191919`. Hover state shifts border to `#93C5FD` with a soft sky wash fill (`#E0F2FE`).
- **Accent / Highlight Action**: Pill shape, background `#3B82F6`, text `#FFFFFF`. Used selectively for deployment executions and active cluster provisioning.

### Chips & Status Indicators
- **Live Status Badges**: Pill shape, height 24px, padding 2px 10px, background `#E0F2FE`, border 1px solid `#BAE6FD`. Displays a 6px circular indicator dot: `#3B82F6` (Active / Optimizing), `#60A5FA` (Healthy / Ready), `#F59E0B` (Thermal Warning), `#EF4444` (Critical Alert). Text set in `label-tag` with `#1D4ED8`.
- **Filter Tags**: Corner radius 4px, height 28px, background `#F0EFE9`, text `#4E4E4A`. Selected state shifts to `#E0F2FE` background, `#3B82F6` text, and `#93C5FD` border.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Radius 4px, height 40px, padding 10px 14px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#DEDDD5`, placeholder `#9E9D95`. Focus state triggers an ice blue border (`#3B82F6`) with a 3px soft ambient sky halo (`rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 16px square or circular bounds, 1px solid `#DEDDD5`. Checked state fills `#3B82F6` with `#FFFFFF` checkmark or center pip.

### Cards & Telemetry Containers
- **Compute Architecture Card**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#DEDDD5`, radius 6px, padding 24px. Features an optional atmospheric header gradient (`linear-gradient(180deg, #E0F2FE 0%, #FFFFFF 35%)`) to spotlight high-priority node telemetry.
- **Log / Telemetry Well**: Recessed background `#F0EFE9`, border 1px solid `#E8E7E0`, radius 4px, padding 16px. Geist monospace values with ice blue metadata accents (`#3B82F6`) for active timestamps.

### Lists & Tables
- **Data Rows**: 1px solid `#E8E7E0` bottom border, 12px vertical padding. Hover state introduces a luminous soft blue-tinted fill (`rgba(224, 242, 254, 0.5)`). Numeric indicators and GPU load bars utilize `#3B82F6` with tabular figures.
